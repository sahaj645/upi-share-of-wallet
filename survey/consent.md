# Consent (survey and interview)

We are students researching how college students use UPI apps, for the Paytm UPI
Growth Challenge. This is voluntary academic research.

- Your participation is **voluntary**. You can skip any question or stop at any
  time.
- We collect your answers about **how and why** you pay, and figures from your
  UPI history (amounts, type, app). **We do not record your name, phone number,
  email, UPI ID, or take un-blurred screenshots.**
- Responses are stored **anonymously** and reported only in aggregate (and as
  short anonymised quotes, e.g. "F, 2nd yr, hostel, G2").
- Nothing here is a Paytm product or offer. The concept at the end is a
  hypothetical idea we are testing, not a real feature.

By continuing, you confirm you are a college student and consent to take part on
these terms.
