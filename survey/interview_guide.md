# In-depth interview guide (15–20 min)

Record their exact words, mixed-language included. Anonymise as a profile only
(e.g. "F, 2nd yr, hostel, Bihar, G2"). Never record names, numbers, or UPI IDs.

## 0. Consent (read first)

Read `survey/consent.md`. Confirm verbal consent and that they can stop anytime.

## 1. Payment walk-through

"Show me your UPI history. For the last 10 payments: amount, where, which app,
and why that app?"

Log each in the same four fields as the form: **amount, type, app, why**.

## 2. Unprompted barrier

"For everyday payments, what's the one reason you don't use Paytm?"

Do **not** prompt or suggest reasons. Follow up: "Tell me about the last time."

## 3. Switching story

"Have you ever changed your main app? Take me through what happened."

Capture: from → to, roughly when, and the trigger.

## 4. G1 only (Paytm-primary)

"Why Paytm? Has anything almost made you leave?"

## 5. Money flow

"How does money reach you each month?" (allowance / on request / own income /
scholarship / mix; and via which channel — UPI/app, bank, cash)

## 6. Neutral concept

Read the concept card verbatim (see `questionnaire.md` Section 5). Then ask:
- What would concern you about it?
- What would make you use it?
- Visibility preference: each payment / weekly totals / only total?
- Who has to agree: you / parent / both?

## Wrap-up

Thank them. Confirm nothing identifying was written down. Save notes using the
front-matter template in `data/raw/interviews/` (gitignored).
