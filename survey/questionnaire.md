# Survey questionnaire (Track A — primary-app preference)

Order matters. Open questions come first, closed categories later, and the
concept card comes last, so nothing earlier steers the answers. No one is
dropped for being Paytm-primary; screening answers are kept for rates only.

---

## Section 1 · Screening (answers kept for rates; no one is dropped)

1. **Are you currently a college student?** (Yes / No)
   - No → thank and end.
2. **Have you made a UPI payment in the last 30 days?** (Yes / No)
   - No → record, thank and end.
3. **Is Paytm installed on your phone?** (Yes / No)
   - No → short path: ask Q5 (primary app) + one open question ("What is the
     main reason Paytm is not on your phone?"), then end. Used for rates only.
4. **Have you made a UPI payment with Paytm in the last 90 days?** (Yes / No)
5. **Which app did you use for MOST of your UPI payments in the last month?**
   (PhonePe / GPay / Paytm / BHIM / Other)

**Group assignment (automatic — not shown to respondent):**
- Paytm primary (Q5 = Paytm) → **G1**
- Another app primary AND Paytm used in 90 days (Q4 = Yes) → **G2**
- Another app primary AND Paytm not used in 90 days (Q4 = No) → **G3**

---

## Section 2 · Unprompted questions (open text, no options shown)

6. For everyday payments, what is the single biggest reason you use **[your
   primary app]** instead of Paytm?
   *(G1 respondents see: "…use Paytm instead of other apps?")*
7. When did you last use Paytm, and for what?
8. Have you ever switched your primary UPI app? If yes, from which app to which,
   roughly when, and what caused the switch?

---

## Section 3 · Last 10 UPI payments (open your UPI history)

For each of the last 10 payments, four quick fields:

| Field | Input |
|-------|-------|
| Amount (₹) | number |
| Type | Merchant / P2P (friend/family) / Bill / Recharge / Other |
| App used | PhonePe / GPay / Paytm / BHIM / Other |
| Why that app | short open text |

This lets us compute Paytm share by count and by ₹, split into small
(≤ ₹500) and large payments. The "Why" field shows whether the merchant, a
friend, or the student decided.

## Section 4 · Context (closed)

9.  Year of study (1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th / 5th+)
10. Hostel or day scholar? (Hostel / Day scholar)
11. Home state (open / dropdown)
12. College (open)
13. Has a UPI payment failed in the last 3 months? Which app? What did you do
    next? (Yes + app + open / No)
14. How is your monthly spending mostly funded? (Parents' regular allowance /
    Parents send when asked / Own income / Scholarship / Mix)
15. How does money from parents reach you? (UPI — which app / Bank transfer /
    Cash / NA)
16. Does either parent use Paytm? (Yes — for what / No / Not sure)
17. How often do you split payments with friends in a week? (0 / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6+)

---

## Section 5 · Neutral concept test (last)

> "Imagine Paytm allowed a parent to give a student a monthly spending limit
> from the parent's bank account. The student could make UPI payments within
> that limit."

18. Would you use it? (1–5)
19. What would concern you about it? (open)
20. What would make you use it? (open)
21. Would you prefer your parent to see each payment, weekly category totals, or
    only the total? (Each payment / Weekly totals / Only total)
22. Who would need to agree to set it up: you, your parent, or both?
    (You / Parent / Both)
